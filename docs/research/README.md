# Research

Market and ecosystem research: how shells actually gain and lose users,
what people say they hate about the one they have, who else is in the
quadrant, and what a launch and a distribution story have to get right.

These pages exist so the roadmap argues from evidence rather than from
memory. **They are research, not measurement** — the surveys here were
taken in August 2026, they describe other projects and the wider
ecosystem, and nothing in them is a measurement of this repository. A
number about this shell's own behavior belongs to a `make` target, not
to this directory.
